VentoyDroid Privacy Policy

App: VentoyDroid (sh.ventoydroid.app) · Developer: sgateori · Last updated: 2026-10-22

VentoyDroid installs, updates, repairs and restores Ventoy on a USB stick over USB OTG, and copies ISO and other files to and from the stick. It has no accounts, shows no ads and sells no data. It is an unofficial app. This page covers the Google Play version.

What stays on your device

The app works on your USB stick and on files you pick. It reaches files on the phone only through Android's system file picker, and each USB stick only after you approve Android's USB permission dialog. The Ventoy boot images are bundled in the app. The app itself downloads nothing: it contains no code that fetches Ventoy or ISO files. You download those yourself with a browser and then pick the file. Its three About links (ventoy.net, the licence text and the source repository) open in your browser. Android backup and device transfer are switched off for the app. Uninstalling it deletes its local data.

Installing or restoring erases the whole stick. The app names the drive and its size and asks for a confirmation before it does this.

Network use

The Google Play version has network access only so that Firebase can upload reports if you switch them on or send a log (see below). Nothing else in the app opens a network connection.

Crash reports and anonymous usage (Firebase)

To fix crashes and see how installs go, the Play version includes Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics. Google acts as our processor. This is off by default. Nothing is uploaded until you turn on Send crash and usage reports in About. Turning it off stops both services and also deletes crashes that were recorded but not yet sent. It is always off in debug builds.

Two details: crashes recorded while the switch was off are uploaded when you turn it on, and the Send log button (below) works even with the switch off, and when tapped it also releases any crash kept on the phone.

VentoyDroid does not deliberately send drive serial numbers, volume labels, ISO or file names or contents, file addresses, location or accounts in these events or crash keys.

The Firebase libraries also collect some standard data on their own: device model, operating system and app version, language, general events such as first open, sessions and screen views, and random per-install identifiers (the Firebase app-instance ID and the Crashlytics installation ID). Google derives an approximate country or region from your IP address when the data arrives. These identifiers are not your advertising ID. VentoyDroid does not use the advertising ID, and its AD_ID permission is removed.

Send log

In the Play version, About has a Send log button. Only when you tap it, the app attaches its own last 400 log lines and uploads them once to Firebase Crashlytics, together with any crash kept on the phone. The app replaces content:// and file:// addresses in the log, but nothing else is removed, so the log can include file and drive names. Do not tap it if you do not want that. This works even when the usage switch is off.

Limit: if the app crashes with an unexpected error while reports are on, the error text written by the system or a library is sent as it is, and in rare cases it could include a drive label or a file name. We filter what the app itself reports, but we cannot filter text produced inside the crash.

Data is encrypted in transit. It is kept by Firebase under Google's default retention. See Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

What we do not collect

We do not receive the contents of your USB stick or ISO files, drive serial numbers, volume labels, accounts, contacts, messages, photos, or your location from your device, and we run no server that stores such data. We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

Permissions

Internet and network state (Firebase reports only; Play version); foreground service of type data sync and wake lock (keep an install, a file copy or a persistence-file write running with a visible notification, since stopping half way can leave the stick unbootable); notifications (progress). USB host is optional hardware access, granted per stick through Android's own dialog. The app requests no storage, location, Bluetooth, contacts or microphone permission. A separate free-software build exists that has no network permission and no Firebase code; it is not the Google Play version.

Your choices and deleting data

Reports are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off again at any time, which also deletes unsent reports on the phone. We have no account system, so we cannot look up your data by name. To ask us to delete reports from your install, email us the installation identifier or a description of when the crash happened, and we will delete what we can match. Uninstalling removes all local data.

Children

VentoyDroid is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children.
