Audio Converter Privacy Policy

App: Audio Converter (com.sat.flac2mp3) · Developer: sgateori · Last updated: 2026-10-29

Audio Converter converts audio files on your phone (for example FLAC, WAV or AIFF to MP3, M4A, FLAC or WAV). It has no accounts, shows no ads and has no server of ours. This page says what leaves your device.

What stays on your device

The audio files you pick, their names, tags, cover art, folder names and paths, and the converted output are handled only on your phone. Conversion happens on the device. You choose input files or a folder with the system file picker; the app declares no storage permission. Output goes to Music/FLAC2MP3 or to a folder you choose. Settings may be included in your own Android backup. The local crash log (below) is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.

Network connections

The app's own code does not open network connections. The Internet permission is used only by the Firebase libraries described next.

Crash reports and usage statistics (Firebase)

The app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to find crashes and see which features are used. Firebase acts as our service provider and processes this data on our behalf; we do not sell it or use it for advertising.

This is off by default. The first time you open the app it asks once, with two equal buttons, Share and No thanks. Nothing is collected until you choose Share. You can change your choice at any time with one switch, Share crash reports and usage statistics, in Settings. It covers both services. Collection is never active in debug builds.

The code does not send file names, folder names, paths, tags, cover art, audio, or exact sizes and times (ranges only). Input formats are recognised from the file extension against a fixed list, otherwise reported as "other".

Limit: if the app crashes with an unexpected error, the error text produced by Android or a library is sent as it is. The app does not filter it, and in rare cases such text could include a file name or path. We therefore say the app does not deliberately send file names or paths, not that it never can.

Google also derives an approximate country or region from your IP address when Analytics data arrives. The app never reads your location and has no location permission.

See Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy. Data is encrypted in transit.

Local crash log and email report

The app writes a crash log file (stack trace, device model, Android version, thread name) to its private storage. It leaves your phone only if you tap Email report, which opens your own mail app addressed to sgateori@proton.me; you can review and edit the message before sending.

What we do not collect

Your advertising ID, location, contacts, audio, files, file names or paths (see the limit above), account or personal information. We have no accounts or sign-in.

Permissions

Internet and network state (used by Firebase only); notifications (conversion progress and completion; conversion works if you decline); foreground service of type data sync (keeps a batch you started running with the screen off). Android may stop a data-sync service after about 6 hours in a day; the app then cancels the batch and stops. No storage, location, contacts, camera, microphone or all-files access.

Your choices and deletion

Use the Settings switch to stop collection at any time. Uninstalling removes local data. We have no account system, so we cannot look up data by name. To ask us to delete reports from your install, email us the Firebase installation identifier or a description of when a crash happened, and we will delete what we can match.

Children

Audio Converter is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has sent us data, email us and we will delete what we can match.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version replaces this page.
